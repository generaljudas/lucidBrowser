# ADR-NNNN: Title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation forces a decision, and which goals or invariants it touches.
Write the tension, not just the topic.

## Decision

What we chose, stated so a reader could re-implement the choice without
asking. One decision per ADR.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative, with the honest reason it lost. If nothing else was
seriously considered, the decision probably did not need an ADR.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we are now committed to.
Include the uncomfortable consequences — they are the ones that matter later.
